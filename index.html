A quick reference

Browse smarter. Play safer.

The useful part is finding a resource. The important part is knowing when to trust an offer.

01

Match the offer to your game.

Confirm the game name and any requirements. A dice link, a gift link, and a reward code may each have a different redemption process.

02

Expect limits, not guarantees.

Promotions can expire or be restricted to eligible accounts. Previously claimed offers may not work again, even when a link still opens.

03

Keep control of your account.

Never share your password or pay an unfamiliar website to release a free gift. Avoid unofficial downloads and verify suspicious offers with the publisher.

Directory notes

Why is the same game listed twice?

Match Masters has two gift resources. Monopoly GO has separate destinations for dice rewards and reward codes. The resource labels help you tell these entries apart.

Does GameResources provide the rewards?

No. This is a directory of external resources. We do not issue rewards, generate game currency, or handle redemptions. Offers depend on the game publisher and your eligibility.

Are these official game resources?

GameResources is independent and is not endorsed by the publishers. The listed domains are external websites; verify unfamiliar offers against official game channels.

What if a reward link does not work?

The offer may have expired, may already have been claimed, or may have account restrictions. Do not pay to unlock it or share your login details to make it work.
